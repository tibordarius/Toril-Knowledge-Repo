# Inference and Coupling Architecture

The extraction pipeline separates **source-grounded systems** from **derived inference**. System layers may organize explicit evidence into flows, dependencies, capacities, shocks or couplings. Inference layers may derive new claims, but those claims must remain labeled as inferred and carry reasoning, assumptions and uncertainty.

## Cross-domain reasoning contract

A cross-domain chain should remain decomposed into atomic records. Example:

```text
drought
  -> reduces crop yield
  -> increases grain scarcity
  -> raises import demand
  -> stresses port and caravan capacity
  -> raises prices
  -> increases urban hardship
  -> raises political pressure
```

The model should preserve each step, its evidence class, time window, scale and uncertainty. Do not jump directly from the first event to the final political consequence without the intermediate mechanisms.

## Preferred interaction lenses

- economy ↔ politics
- economy ↔ military
- demography ↔ labor
- ecology ↔ economy
- geography ↔ logistics
- infrastructure ↔ trade
- magic ↔ technology
- religion ↔ politics
- crime ↔ governance
- information ↔ power
- disease ↔ trade
- climate ↔ agriculture

These are not assumptions that a coupling exists. They are prompts to check whether the evidence supports one.
